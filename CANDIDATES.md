# Scariest sentence candidates (draft pass)

One sentence each, under 200 characters, judged on how
frightening, vivid and original it is. No gore; dread only.

1. Baby monitor: "The voice on the baby monitor laughed at
   your joke before you finished telling it downstairs." --
   anticipation is the horror: whatever it is, it knows how
   the joke ends.
2. The cabin: "When the search party found the cabin, the
   dinner was still warm, the chairs were still rocking,
   and every mirror in the house was facing the wall." --
   three clauses of evidence and one ritual detail; the
   most vivid of the set, and the mirrors give the reader
   the final turn to make alone.
3. Voicemail: "Her phone had one new voicemail, from her own
   number, timestamped tomorrow." -- clean, classic shape.
4. Elevator: "The elevator stopped at a floor the building
   doesn't have, and someone inside the walls said, 'Yours
   is next.'" -- direct address threat.
5. Dog: "The dog stared at the corner of the empty room,
   wagging slowly, the way she only wags for family." --
   quiet and domestic.

## Verdict

Winner: candidate 2. The judging is on frightening, vivid
and original, and candidate 2 wins vivid outright: warm
dinner, rocking chairs -- and then the detail that turns
evidence into ritual, every mirror facing the wall, which
tells the reader the people in that cabin knew what was
coming and had a practice for it. The baby monitor
(candidate 1) is the better single mechanism; the cabin is
the sentence that stays in the room after you leave it.
